Explore the clear, cold Radovna River and the glacial Krma Valley. Lake Kreda is a possible stop on the way; check swimming conditions and access locally. The pin marks Lake Kreda.

Stop at Kovinarska koča and Pr’ Psnak for traditional homemade food.
