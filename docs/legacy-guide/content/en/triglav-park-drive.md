Bled → Kranjska Gora → Vršič Pass → Trenta Valley → Bovec → Kobarid → Most na Soči → Bohinj → Bled. A car train runs from Most na Soči towards Bohinj; check the current timetable and arrive ahead of departure, as service is not guaranteed. If necessary, return by road over Soriška planina.
